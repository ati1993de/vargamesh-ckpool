# Upstream

VargaMesh CKPool is based on `ckpool-lhr`.

Upstream repository:

https://github.com/Z3r0XG/ckpool-lhr

The VargaMesh production development line was based on upstream commit:

`b51d54b448d50b3ec3399d48c647ef30b0f9cb2f`

`ckpool-lhr` itself is derived from CKPool by Con Kolivas.

VargaMesh-specific changes include integration required for the VMESH
SHA256/AuxPoW mining workflow and the VargaMesh AuxPoW bridge.

Original copyright notices, GPL licensing information and bundled
third-party license information are intentionally preserved.
